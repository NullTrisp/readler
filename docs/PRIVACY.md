# Publication and privacy checklist

This is a checklist for the final hosted privacy policy, not legal advice.

- Explain that Readler accesses the user-selected Drive folder in read-only mode.
- Explain that progress and bookmarks are stored locally and in Drive's hidden `appDataFolder`.
- State that book contents and reading data are not sent to Readler-operated servers.
- Explain that the web app stores imported books and manual downloads in the browser's IndexedDB and that clearing site data removes them.
- Document how to disconnect Google, revoke access, and delete synchronized data from Settings.
- Publish support and privacy URLs before OAuth review and store submission.
- Provide an OAuth verification video showing sign-in, folder selection, reading, synchronization, and deletion.
- Complete the Play Data Safety and App Store Privacy disclosures consistently with the policy.
- Replace placeholder icons, screenshots, store descriptions, and OAuth branding before release.
